Question 01

What was the first thing you paid attention to when interacting with the experience?

My very first impression when entering the “Luni website” was how colourful and cute the home page is. They employ 3D rendering techniques to create navigable, clickable areas within their company premises—spaces you can interact with using a mouse or touchpad. They opted for a summer-inspired color palette dominated by purple, complemented by yellow, pink, and red; the combination is visually appealing and instantly lifts the user's mood upon their first visit to the site. I arrived at this conclusion after exploring every website on my selected list.

Question 02

What discrete actions did you perform during the first two minutes?

  1. Used the touchpad to rotate the 3D models on the homepage.
  2. Clicked on specific sections of the homepage: Omada, Studio, Career, and Luni Life.
  3. Explored the pages linked to those sections.
  4. Read the introductions and information provided in each category.
  5. Swiped up or down to view additional information.
  6. Viewed photos of the company and its staff featured on each page.
  7. Visited the company's external profiles via links on the site.

Question 03

What part of the experience did you spend the most time engaging with?

I mostly spend my time reading and viewing the information they display on the website. The 3D models on the homepage are the special feature that attracted my interest the most, observing them inspired me to dig deeper into the site.

Question 04

What was the most common action in your two-minute interaction?

I spent most of my time clicking on their showcase categories—specifically the 3D models—because of the website's playful visual appearance. It’s an experience few other websites offer.

Question 05

What is your impression of the intended primary goal of the interactive experience?

I believed the primary goal of the website is to introduce the creativeness and the identity of their careers as well as showcase their work through the interactive and interesting activity through out the website. Instead of just simply presents all the information, they required audiences to directly interact with the website in order to view all of the hidden information behind the 3D rendering models.

Question 06

How does the interactive experience communicate this primary goal?

By using vibrant, striking colors—such as purple, pink, red, and yellow—they express their creative work in an abstract way. They incorporate interactive elements that compel visitors to engage with the site, sparking curiosity and interest that encourage users to explore further and view all the posted content and imagery. This approach sets them apart from other websites. In my opinion, Luni has truly succeeded in communicating the company's personality and establishing their expertise in the field of interactive media.

Question 07

How should the experience be interacted with over time?

I believe this website is designed for a quick visit—taking just 3 to 8 minutes to explore—as it doesn't have an overwhelming number of sections, and the content is concise enough to keep readers engaged without causing information overload or boredom. However, the site also encourages users to return and experience the 3D spaces—an engaging feature well worth the time—as well as to stay updated on any new information posted in the future.

Question 08

How does the experience communicate how it should be interacted with over time?

The use of 3D models, interactive effects, and multiple areas to explore encourages users to stay longer or return later. Notably, the inclusion of the message "We're building the next generation of genre-defining brands" amidst the homepage's 3D models reinforces the intent to have users come back to witness the company's future innovations through the experiences and information provided on the site.

Question 09

What other media forms does the experience reference?

This website strongly reminds me of dollhouse models— something that I have absolutely loved since childhood and still do today; that is why I chose this site, as it evokes my fondest memories. The 3D office setup is adorable, and users can use the touchpad to rotate the view and closely examine details like the furniture, the play area, and so on.

Question 10

What do these references communicate about how you should act?

Those references immediately sparked a desire in me to explore further and see what else lay within—to discover if there were other models hidden deeper inside. Feelings of curiosity, eagerness, and excitement surged the moment I laid eyes on those 3D models. That engagement actually drove me to read through the accompanying text—something I rarely do, as I usually find reading web content tedious and uninspiring, often causing me to lose interest in exploring further.

Question 11

What do these references communicate about how you should feel?

The memories of the dollhouse evoked by this website brought up the feeling of nostalgia, playful and truly comfortable as it reminds me of my childhood. At the same time, the miniature 3D models sparked a sense of wonder and excitement, creating a delightful experience that made reading the site's textual content engaging and enjoyable. This combination make the website becomes more like a place to explore rather than a simple soure of company's informartion.

Question 12

What was the most frustrating part of the interaction?

The most frustrating aspect of the website experience is that it sometimes requires too many steps, leaving me unsure whether to click, rotate, or swipe to access different information. Many clickable elements blend into the background, and sections requiring a swipe to view more content lack clear visual cues, causing confusion about what to do next.

Question 13

What was the most satisfying part of the interaction?

I am convinced that the most satisfying aspect of interacting with the website is exploring its various areas via 3D models, I can rotate and zoom in on details, and click directly on the model to navigate to the corresponding information page—an experience that feels far from lazy compared to my previous encounters with websites.
